# Design

Visual direction: a quiet chess-club study table, with the board as the primary object. The memorable element is the board-first layout and physical green/brass palette.

Colors:
- Ink #17211d
- Paper #f7f3ea
- Panel #fffdf7
- Board green #55745d
- Brass #b8893d
- Alert red #9d3f35

Typography:
- System sans for interface text
- Georgia for large page headings

UI rules:
- Keep board large and visually dominant.
- Cards use 8px radius and restrained borders.
- Buttons use lucide icons where helpful.
- Mobile places the board before dense information.
- Focus states must stay visible.
- Do not introduce neon, heavy gradients, glass effects, or decorative clutter.


Board update, 2026-08-12:
- The playable board uses a chess.com-inspired classic green palette: light #eeeed2 and dark #769656.
- Pieces are original inline SVG Staunton-style pieces, not proprietary chess.com assets.
- Coordinates match square color contrast and pieces are sized to avoid clipping.
